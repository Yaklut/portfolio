Featured Projects

Kimia Farma Performance Analytics (2020–2023)

Project-Based Virtual Internship — Big Data Analytics · Kimia Farma × Rakamin Academy · Apr 17–May 15, 2025

Context: Kimia Farma, one of Indonesia's largest pharmaceutical companies, needed a data-driven way to evaluate business performance across its branch network.

My contribution: I imported and integrated four datasets — transactions, products, inventory, and branch offices — into Google BigQuery covering 2020–2023. I wrote SQL queries using CTEs, LEFT JOINs, and CASE WHEN logic to compute nett sales and gross profit margins by product price tier, then built a Looker Studio dashboard to surface the results.

Bank Muamalat (BMI) Sales Performance Dashboard

Project-Based Virtual Internship — Business Intelligence Analyst · Bank Muamalat × Rakamin Academy · Oct 6–Nov 3, 2025

Context: Bank Muamalat needed a data-driven way to evaluate sales performance across products, customer segments, and cities.

My contribution: I mapped entity relationships and identified primary keys across four relational datasets (Customer, Orders, Product, Product Category), then constructed a master table in BigQuery using multi-table SQL JOINs, and built a sales performance dashboard with strategic recommendations on product portfolio segmentation, geographic marketing, and data-driven customer retention via CRM.
